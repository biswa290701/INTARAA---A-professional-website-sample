About Us

Intaraa is an independently developed product by Inexa Studios, created to make learning and skill-building more interactive, immersive, and effective.

Intaraa uses lifelike 3D characters and real-world conversation scenarios to help students, trainees, and the workforce practice and refine a wide range of competencies. From medical and clinical training to infrastructure, safety, communication, and professional skill development, it provides a realistic simulation environment that enhances understanding and improves performance.

Designed for both individual learners and organizations, Intaraa integrates seamlessly into LMS platforms, courses, certification programs, and custom training workflows. Whether you are enhancing an existing curriculum or building an end-to-end learning program, INTARAA adapts to your needs and elevates the overall training experience.

At Inexa Studios, we believe that impactful learning happens when training feels real — and Intaraa brings that realism to every scenario.
